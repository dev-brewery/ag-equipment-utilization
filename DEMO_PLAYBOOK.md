# RentalIQ Demo Playbook
## "Show Me the Money" Sales Presentation

### Core Principle: Use THEIR Data to Show THEIR Lost Money

**Key Insight:** Generic demos don't sell. Dealers need to see their own money being left on the table.

**Demo Duration:** 45 minutes
**Preparation Time:** 2-4 hours (data load + analysis)
**Close Rate Target:** 60%+ to pilot, 80%+ pilot-to-paid conversion

---

## Pre-Demo Preparation (Critical for Success)

### Step 1: Qualify the Lead (15 min phone call)

**Qualification Questions:**
1. How many locations do you have? *(Need: 2+)*
2. What's your approximate annual rental revenue? *(Need: $250K+)*
3. Do you use a dealer management system? Which one? *(Note: for integration planning)*
4. Can you export your rental history to CSV or Excel? *(Need: Yes)*
5. What's your biggest frustration with rental operations? *(Discovery)*

**Disqualifiers:**
- Single location (no inter-location optimization opportunity)
- <$100K annual rental revenue (too small for ROI)
- No rental history data available
- Not decision-maker and can't bring decision-maker to demo

**If Qualified → Send Pre-Demo Packet:**

**Email Template:**
```
Subject: Prepare for Your RentalIQ Demo - Action Required

Hi [Name],

I'm looking forward to showing you how much rental revenue you're currently leaving
on the table - and exactly how to capture it.

To make this demo relevant to YOUR business (not a generic demo), I need you to
send me the following data exports from your dealer management system:

REQUIRED:
1. Rental History - Last 12 months (CSV or Excel)
   Fields needed: Equipment ID, Start Date, End Date, Daily Rate, Location

2. Equipment Inventory - Current (CSV or Excel)
   Fields needed: Equipment ID, Type, Make/Model, Location, Acquisition Cost

OPTIONAL (but makes demo better):
3. Transfer History - If you track it
4. Maintenance Records

it won't show YOUR specific opportunities.

Deadline: 2 business days before demo

Questions? Call me: [Phone]

Looking forward to showing you some insights you've never seen before,
[Your Name]
```

## Demo Script (45 Minutes)

### Opening (5 minutes)

**Hook - Start with the Number:**

> "Thanks for joining me today. I'm going to show you something that might be uncomfortable:
> **how much money you're losing every month on equipment that's sitting idle.**
>
> Based on the data you sent me, you left approximately **$[XX,XXX] on the table last year.**
> That's not a guess - that's calculated from your actual rental history.
>
> In the next 45 minutes, I'm going to show you:
> 1. Exactly WHERE you're losing that money (equipment-by-equipment)
> 2. WHY it's happening (wrong location, wrong price, seasonal, etc.)
> 3. WHAT to do about it (specific actions you can take this week)
> 4. HOW to know if it's working (tracking and proof)
>
> Sound good? Let me share my screen..."

**Set Expectations:**
- "This is YOUR data, not generic demo data"
- "I'm going to show you things you've never seen before"
- "Feel free to interrupt with questions"
- "At the end, I'll propose a 90-day free pilot to prove this works"

### Part 1: Lost Revenue Dashboard (15 minutes)

**Screen: Main Dashboard**

> "Okay, here's your RentalIQ dashboard. This is what you'd see when you log in each morning.
>
> **[Point to top metric]**
> This number here - **$[XX,XXX]** - that's how much revenue you lost in the last 12 months
> because equipment was idle when it could have been rented.
>
> Now, you might be thinking 'Well, equipment can't be rented 100% of the time, that's normal.'
> You're absolutely right. But here's the thing..."

**Screen: Utilization Breakdown**

> "Your current utilization rate is **[XX]%**. The industry average for dealers like you is
> **45-50%**. The top performers hit **55-60%**.
>
> If we can get you from [current]% to just [target]%, that's an additional **$[XX,XXX] per year**
> in revenue with the SAME equipment you already own. No new purchases needed.
>
> Let me show you WHERE this opportunity is hiding..."

**Screen: Worst Performers Table**

> "Here's your Top 10 worst-performing equipment. This one here - **[Equipment Type #ID]** -
> sat idle for **47 days last year** when it could have been rented.
>
> At your average daily rate of $[XXX], that's **$[XX,XXX]** you could have made.
>
> But here's the kicker: **[Click to expand details]**
>
> During those 47 idle days, your [Other Location] had **8 customer requests** for this exact
> type of equipment. You literally had customers asking for it at one location while it sat
> idle at another.
>
> **[Point to reason breakdown chart]**
> Here's WHY you lost that revenue:
> - 65% was due to being at the wrong location ($[X,XXX])
> - 20% was seasonal low demand - not much you can do about that
> - 15% was pricing too high based on local market rates
>
> See how it breaks down what's actionable vs. what's just reality?
>
> **[Look at dealer]** Does this match what you've suspected, or is this surprising?"

**Pause for Reaction & Questions**

*Common dealer reactions:*
- **"I had no idea..."** → Great, that's the value
- **"We thought this was happening..."** → Now you have proof and can quantify it
- **"This equipment is at Location A because..."** → Perfect, that local knowledge is valuable

> "Exactly. And this is why we built RentalIQ to be TRANSPARENT. You can see the calculation
> methodology, you can override our recommendations when you have local knowledge we don't.
>
> The algorithm gets smarter when you tell it what it got wrong.
>
> Let me show you what to DO about this..."

### Part 2: Smart Transfer Recommendations (10 minutes)

**Screen: Transfer Queue**

> "Every day, RentalIQ analyzes your entire fleet across all locations and asks:
> 'Is there equipment sitting idle at Location A that's needed at Location B?'
>
> Here are today's opportunities:
>
> **[Click on top recommendation]**
>
> **URGENT: Transfer [Equipment #ID] from [Location A] to [Location B]**
>
> Let me walk you through why this is a good move..."

**Screen: Transfer Detail View**

> "Here's how we calculate the ROI on this transfer:
>
> **Expected Revenue at Location B:** $[XX,XXX]
> - You have 2 customers who've already inquired about this equipment
> - Based on historical demand at Location B this time of year, we estimate 6 rental days
> - At $[XXX]/day, that's $[X,XXX]
> - We apply a 75% probability (based on similar situations in the past)
>
> **Transport Cost:** $[XXX]
> - Distance: [XX] miles
> - Your per-mile rate: $[X]/mile (we pulled this from your QuickBooks)
> - Driver time: [X] hours at $[XX]/hour
>
> **Opportunity Cost:** $[XXX]
> - What revenue you might lose by moving it FROM Location A
> - Right now: very low, no pending requests there
>
> **NET ROI: $[X,XXX]**
>
> **Payback: 3 days** - meaning in 3 days of rentals, you've covered the transport cost
>
> **[Point to accuracy tracker]**
> Now here's the trust-builder: We track how often we're right.
>
> This quarter, we've recommended 23 transfers to our pilot dealers. 19 were profitable,
> 3 broke even, 1 lost money. Average ROI when accepted: $[X,XXX].
>
> So we're not perfect, but we're right about 83% of the time.
>
> **[Look at dealer]**
> If you saw this recommendation this morning, what would you do? Would you approve it?
> Do you have a driver available? Is there something we're missing?"

**Pause for Discussion**

*This is critical - get them THINKING about execution*

> "This is where the rubber meets the road. The algorithm can recommend, but YOU know your
> business. Maybe you know that customer who inquired is a flake. Maybe your driver called
> in sick. That's fine - you can decline it and tell us why.
>
> The system learns from that feedback.
>
> But imagine having this visibility EVERY DAY. No more phone tag between locations asking
> 'Hey, do you have a skid steer available?' You just look at the dashboard."

### Part 3: Demand Forecasting (10 minutes)

**Screen: 14-Day Demand Forecast**

> "Now let me show you something even more powerful: predicting the future.
>
> This is your demand forecast for the next 14 days, by equipment type.
>
> **[Point to capacity vs. demand chart]**
>
> See this? Next week, you're forecasted to have **18 days of demand** for Skid Steers,
> but you only have **14 days of capacity** (2 units × 7 days).
>
> That means you're going to turn away customers. We estimate **$[X,XXX]** in lost revenue.
>
> But here's the cool part: you can see this AHEAD of time.
>
> **What could you do with this information?**
> - Transfer a skid steer from another location that's going to be slow
> - Rent a unit from a competitor and re-rent it to your customer (still profitable)
> - Call customers who've rented from you before and offer it to them proactively
> - Adjust pricing up (when demand exceeds supply)
>
> **[Click to show forecast methodology]**
>
> How does it know? We combine:
> - Your historical rental patterns (last 3 years, same time of year)
> - Weather forecast (7-day outlook from NOAA)
> - Local agricultural calendar (planting season starts next week in your area)
> - Real-time signals (you have 3 pending reservation inquiries right now)
>
> **[Point to confidence score]**
> And we tell you how confident we are. This forecast is 78% confidence - pretty reliable.
>
> Over time, as we track actual vs. predicted, the algorithm gets better.
>
> **[Look at dealer]**
> If you knew a week ago that you'd be short on skid steers next week, what would you have done?"

**Pause for Discussion**

*Dealers will start thinking strategically*

> "Exactly. This is the difference between being REACTIVE - customer calls, you scramble -
> and being PROACTIVE - you see it coming, you position equipment, you call customers first.
>
> That's how you compete with United Rentals. They have big fleets, you have better service

## Demo Variations for Different Audiences

### For Owner/GM (Focus: Money & Strategy)
- Lead with total lost revenue number
- Emphasize ROI and competitive advantage
- Show how it enables growth without buying more equipment
- Compare to competitors who are optimizing

### For Rental Manager (Focus: Daily Operations)
- Lead with transfer recommendations and daily workflow
- Emphasize time savings (no more phone calls between locations)
- Show demand forecast for capacity planning
- Demonstrate approval workflow

### For IT/DMS Manager (Focus: Integration & Security)
- Lead with integration architecture
- Emphasize read-only, non-disruptive approach
- Show data security and SOC 2 compliance
- Demonstrate how it enhances DMS rather than replaces

### For Multi-Location Chain (Focus: Scale & Standardization)
- Lead with network-wide optimization
- Show how it coordinates across all locations
- Emphasize centralized visibility
- Demonstrate scalability to 10, 20, 50 locations

## Common Demo Pitfalls to Avoid

**❌ Using Generic Demo Data**
- Dealers tune out immediately
- No emotional connection to the numbers
- Can't verify accuracy

**✅ Use Their Data**
- Even if messy, it's THEIRS
- They can verify and validate
- Creates "aha!" moments

---

**❌ Focusing on Features**
- "We have 47 different reports!"
- Dealers don't care about features

**✅ Focus on Outcomes**
- "You'll capture $XX,XXX this year"
- "You'll stop losing rentals to competitors"
- "You'll know what equipment to buy next"

---

**❌ Being Overly Technical**
- "Our algorithm uses gradient boosting and..."
- Dealers don't need to understand the math

**✅ Be Transparent but Simple**
- "We look at your history, the weather, and what customers are asking for"
- "We track when we're right and when we're wrong"

---

**❌ Not Handling Objections**
- Ignore pushback and keep presenting
- Defensive about product limitations

**✅ Address Concerns Head-On**
- "That's a fair concern. Here's how we handle that..."
- "You're right, this won't work if... That's why we do a pilot first"

---

**❌ Weak Close**
- "So... let me know if you're interested"
- No clear next step

**✅ Strong Close**
- "Let's start the pilot. I can have you set up by Friday. What's a good time for a 30-minute setup call?"
- Create urgency: "The $[XX,XXX] you're losing isn't getting smaller"

## Conclusion: The Demo is a Diagnosis

Think of the demo like a doctor's visit:

1. **You run tests** (analyze their data)
2. **You show them the x-ray** (lost revenue report)
3. **You explain the diagnosis** (where and why they're losing money)
4. **You prescribe treatment** (transfer recommendations, forecasting)
5. **You propose a trial** (90-day pilot)
6. **You measure results** (track actual ROI)

The demo isn't about showing off features. It's about diagnosing their problem with data they can't dispute, and offering a cure they can verify.

**If you can show a dealer they're losing $50,000/year and you can help them capture even half of that for a $36,000/year investment - they will say yes.**

The key is using THEIR data to make it undeniable.
