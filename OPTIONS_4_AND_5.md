# Option 4: Workshop Facilitator Partnership

## The Core Model

You partner with an established agricultural training center, mission organization, or educational institution that already has infrastructure, students, and marketing. They handle everything you're bad at; you deliver what you're excellent at.

### Your Responsibilities (What You're Good At)

**Workshop Delivery** (8-12 per year, 16-60 days total active work)
- Teach intensive 2-5 day workshops on aquaponics/sustainable food systems
- Hands-on, practical training (your energizing work)
- Small groups (8-15 participants)
- Locations vary (on-site, partner facilities, community centers)

**Curriculum Development** (One-Time, Then Updates)
- Create workshop materials, handouts, exercises
- Design practical demonstrations and build activities
- Develop assessment tools
- Update annually based on feedback (2-4 weeks/year)

**Occasional Train-the-Trainer** (Optional, 2-4 times/year)
- Train partner's staff to deliver basic workshops
- Allows scaling without your direct involvement
- Additional revenue stream

### Partner's Responsibilities (What You're Bad At)

**Marketing & Student Acquisition**
- Promote workshops to their existing audience
- Manage registration and communications
- Handle inquiries and pre-workshop questions
- Build email lists and run campaigns

**Logistics & Operations**
- Secure facilities and equipment
- Manage scheduling and calendar
- Handle payments and refunds
- Provide materials and supplies
- Arrange accommodations if needed

**Administrative Support**
- Customer support before/after workshops
- Follow-up communications
- Certificate/credential management
- Maintain student database
- Handle legal/insurance requirements

**Ongoing Student Support**
- Answer questions after workshop ends
- Provide community forum or group
- Optional: advanced/alumni workshops

**Option C: 70/30 Split (You 70%)**
- You have established reputation/brand

### How to Find Partners (The "Stumbling Across" Method)

**Phase 1: Research Before Moving (Months 1-6)**
- Join Costa Rica expat forums and sustainable ag groups
- Follow CR permaculture organizations on social media
- Email 10-15 organizations: "Moving to CR in 12 months, aquaponics expertise, interested in collaboration"
- Attend virtual events hosted by CR sustainable ag community
- Goal: Get 2-3 warm responses to follow up on

**Phase 2: Arrive and Network (Months 7-12)**
- Visit every organization on your list
- Attend workshops/events as participant (learn their model)
- Volunteer 1-2 days/week at most promising organization
- Join local Rotary, expat business groups, mission networks
- Give FREE 2-hour intro workshop at 3-4 locations (audition yourself)
- Goal: 1-2 solid partnership prospects

**Phase 3: Pilot Partnership (Months 13-18)**
- Propose pilot: "I'll deliver 2 workshops, you handle marketing/logistics, 50/50 split"
- Price conservatively for first workshops ($1,000-1,500/participant)
- Gather testimonials and feedback religiously
- Refine curriculum based on learnings
- Goal: Prove model works, both sides happy

**Phase 4: Formalize and Scale (Months 19-24)**
- Negotiate annual agreement (8-12 workshops/year)
- Adjust split based on pilot results
- Develop 2-3 workshop tracks
- Create systems for curriculum updates
- Goal: Predictable $40K-60K/year income

### Partnership Agreement Essentials

**Must-Have Clauses**:
1. **Revenue split and payment terms** (paid within 30 days of workshop)
2. **Workshop frequency** (minimum 6/year, maximum 12/year)
3. **Responsibilities matrix** (who does what explicitly)
4. **Intellectual property** (you retain curriculum, can use with other partners)
5. **Non-compete** (define what you can/can't do independently)
6. **Cancellation policy** (minimum notice, kill fee if partner cancels <30 days)
7. **Term and renewal** (1-year initial, auto-renew with 90-day out)
8. **Dispute resolution** (mediation in CR)

**Nice-to-Have Clauses**:
- Performance bonuses (extra 5-10% if workshop ratings >4.5/5)
- Cost-of-living adjustments (annual 3-5% rate increase)
- Expansion options (train-the-trainer, curriculum licensing)
- Right of first refusal (partner gets first shot at new workshop topics)

## Side-by-Side Comparison

| Factor | Option 4: Workshop Partnership | Option 5: Hybrid Mission Role |
|--------|-------------------------------|------------------------------|
| **Income Potential** | $40K-90K/year | $43K-68K/year |
| **Income Stability** | Medium (partner-dependent) | High (salary + supplement) |
| **Active Work Time** | 16-60 days/year | 80-150 days/year |
| **Community Integration** | Medium (through workshops) | High (through mission team) |
| **Mission Alignment** | Medium (teaching sustainable ag) | High (directly serving communities) |
| **Flexibility** | High (300+ free days/year) | Medium (part-time = 2-3 free days/week) |
| **Relational Security** | Low-Medium (workshop participants) | High (mission community = surrogate family) |
| **Financial Risk** | Medium (single partner dependency) | Low (two income streams) |
| **Success Probability** | 35-45% | 40-50% |
| **Passive Income Potential** | High (more time to develop) | Medium (less time but mission network helps) |
| **Stress Level** | Low (only work 60-100 days/year) | Medium (regular schedule and commitments) |
| **Requires Finding** | 1 good partner organization | 1 good mission + 1 workshop partner |

## Decision Criteria

### Choose Option 4 (Workshop Partnership) If:
- ✅ You want maximum flexibility and free time (200-300+ days/year)
- ✅ Teaching workshops is SO energizing you could do 8-12/year happily
- ✅ You're confident you can find one excellent partner organization
- ✅ You want to focus heavily on passive income development
- ✅ Financial upside potential matters more than stability
- ✅ You're willing to accept partner-dependency risk
- ✅ Community building can happen outside work structure

### Choose Option 5 (Hybrid Mission Role) If:
- ✅ You want stable base income with upside potential
- ✅ You value being part of a mission team/community
- ✅ You want work that directly serves communities (beyond teaching)
- ✅ You prefer two smaller income streams over one larger variable stream
- ✅ Benefits (health insurance, retirement match) are important
- ✅ You want immediate integration into mission community (relational security)
- ✅ You're willing to work more days/year for greater stability

### Choose BOTH (Parallel Path) If:
- ✅ You have high risk tolerance and energy
- ✅ You pursue mission role first (months 7-15) while developing workshop partnerships
- ✅ If mission role materializes: Great, add workshops as supplement (Option 5)
- ✅ If mission role doesn't materialize by month 15: Pivot fully to workshops (Option 4)
- ✅ This is actually the RECOMMENDED approach - pursue both, see which emerges

## Recommended Approach: Parallel Pursuit

### Why Pursue Both Simultaneously

**Months 1-12** (Before and early after move):
- Research and reach out to BOTH mission organizations AND workshop partner organizations
- Cast wide net - email 10-15 missions AND 10-15 training centers/partners
- During scouting trip, meet with both types of organizations
- Volunteer at most promising mission while also delivering free intro workshops

**Months 13-18** (Decision point):
- If mission role emerges: Accept it, structure as part-time if possible, add workshops (Option 5)
- If workshop partnership emerges first: Accept it, don't pursue mission role unless part-time (Option 4)
- If BOTH emerge: Choose based on which feels better for community/mission alignment
- If NEITHER emerge: Extend search, draw down savings, reassess at month 24

**Why This Works**:
- Maximizes probability one path succeeds
- Doesn't force premature choice
- Both paths build same network (CR mission/sustainable ag community)
- Both leverage same skills (teaching, technical design)
- Free intro workshops help both paths (audition for partners AND missions see your skills)
- Volunteering at mission helps both paths (relationship building AND potential workshop referrals)

**The "Stumbling Across" Reality**:
- Your best opportunity likely WON'T come from systematic search
- It'll come from relationship you build while pursuing something else
- Example: Volunteering at mission → meet partner who runs training center → workshop partnership emerges
- Example: Deliver free workshop → attendee is mission director → mission role emerges
- Pursuing both creates MORE stumbling opportunities
